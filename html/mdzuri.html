03 / WEB DESIGN & USER EXPERIENCE

MDzuri.

Making digital healthcare feel simpler, clearer and more accessible.

FOCUS UX & Web Design
INDUSTRY Digital Healthcare
PLATFORM Responsive Website
01 / INTRODUCTION

Overview

Connecting people with healthcare through a clearer digital experience.

MDzuri connects patients with board-certified healthcare providers through secure video visits, offering convenient care from anywhere.

The project focused on creating a clean, modern and user-friendly website that helps people explore healthcare services with confidence.

My goal was to balance simplicity, accessibility and trust in an experience that feels welcoming rather than overwhelming.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Healthcare should feel accessible, not complicated.

Looking for healthcare online can feel overwhelming when information is difficult to find or the next step is unclear.

The challenge was to create a website that helps users understand the available services while feeling informed, safe and confident.

01

Finding information

Users need to locate relevant healthcare services without navigating through unnecessary complexity.

02

Building confidence

A healthcare website needs to communicate professionalism, clarity and trust.

03

Creating a clear journey

The experience should guide users naturally from exploring services to understanding their next step.

03 / OBJECTIVES

The Goals

A digital healthcare experience that feels intuitive and trustworthy.

  • Create clear and intuitive navigation.
  • Improve accessibility and usability across different devices.
  • Build trust through consistent typography, colour and layout.
  • Make the user journey simple and easy to follow.
04 / USER NEEDS

Designing with trust in mind.

I explored questions around how people search for healthcare online, what makes a website feel trustworthy and which parts of the experience might create uncertainty.

Three priorities guided the design direction.

PRIORITY 01

Simple navigation

Clear structure and intuitive navigation help users find relevant information without feeling overwhelmed.

PRIORITY 02

Building trust

Consistent visual design, readable typography and a professional appearance support a sense of confidence.

PRIORITY 03

Ease of use

Clear content, responsive layouts and a straightforward user journey help make services easier to explore.

05 / DESIGN APPROACH

The Solution

A clean interface that puts clarity first.

I focused on creating a modern, approachable website with clear navigation and a simple visual structure.

By prioritising readable content, consistent components and an intuitive layout, I wanted to make healthcare services easier to understand and explore.

The design also considered different screen sizes to support a consistent experience across devices.

06 / FINAL DESIGN

Healthcare, made easier to explore.

The final design focuses on a clear presentation of healthcare services, straightforward navigation and a welcoming visual identity.

The homepage introduces the platform, while supporting pages explain how the service works and help visitors explore urgent care.

01 / 03 — Homepage

07 / DESIGN VALIDATION

Evaluating the Experience

Clarity and trust are central to the experience.

The design was developed around key usability considerations: whether visitors can find services, understand how the platform works and recognise the next step.

Further usability testing with potential patients would help validate the navigation, accessibility and overall sense of trust.

08 / OUTCOME

The Outcome

A clearer foundation for accessing digital healthcare.

The design brings together simple navigation, accessible content and a consistent visual identity.

The intended impact is to help visitors feel more confident when exploring healthcare services and understanding their options.

Further user research and testing would be needed to measure improvements in usability and engagement.

09 / REFLECTION

What I Learned

Good healthcare design is built on clarity and trust.

This project taught me that web design goes beyond creating something visually appealing.

I learned how important clear navigation, accessibility and a reassuring visual experience are when designing for healthcare.

It helped me focus more on users' needs and the role of thoughtful design in making complex services easier to understand.
