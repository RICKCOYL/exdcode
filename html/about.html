I am a UX-designer creating experiences that just make sense

I’m a UX design student who enjoys creating intuitive and calm digital experiences where users feel in control. I’m drawn to simple, well-structured design, and I like making things feel clear without overcomplicating them.

I enjoy working with user insights and turning them into solutions that actually make sense in real use. For me, it’s about finding the balance between function and feeling, making something that works well but also feels easy and natural to use.

I’m currently looking for opportunities where I can keep growing as a designer, learn from others, and be part of creating thoughtful, user-centered products.
